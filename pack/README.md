# Photon for Horizon

[Photon Shaders](https://github.com/sixthsurge/photon) v1.4 by SixthSurge, ported to Horizon's own shader pack
format (`.hshader`, shader API 0.1).

Photon's atmosphere (its precomputed scattering table), sun, stars and galaxy; its lighting model with soft,
coloured shadows, subsurface scattering on foliage and its block light falloff; its water (waves, absorption,
refraction, Fresnel reflections on Horizon's water mirror); waving plants; volumetric cumulus clouds and a cirrus
sheet; air fog with light shafts; bloom, colour grading, the Lottes tone curve, FXAA and CAS sharpening.

What Horizon cannot run yet, and so is missing: TAA, ambient occlusion beyond vanilla's, screen-space reflections
on land, depth of field, Photon's moon phases, the altocumulus, cumulus congestus and noctilucent layers, the
aurora and rainbows, shading of entities, block entities, the hand and particles (they keep vanilla's shading),
and Photon's Nether and End looks. Weather follows Horizon's clock, not the world's day count.
